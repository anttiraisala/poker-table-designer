Poker Table Designer
Cursor –

Origin (0, 0) is the cloth centre mark (the pink diamond is the artwork origin); X grows to the right and Y grows downward. The mouse wheel zooms toward the pointer. Pan with the right button, Space + left drag, or hold the middle button to move all artwork together. Drag words, lines and cards to move them, drag a rectangle to select several (Shift/Ctrl+click adds), Ctrl+A selects all, arrow keys nudge by 1 mm (Shift 10 mm). Select a table on the Tables tab or click its outline to edit its nodes. Hold Alt or Ctrl while dragging to ignore snapping. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo.

Casino name

The randomizer makes its Split and Monogram name styles from this name. Texts ticked “Part of the casino name” are your own version of it; a name style hides them and puts its own texts in their place.

Things on the table

Pot, burn cards and muck, and the players of each table (Tables tab). They are shown in the picture and built as 3D objects in Blender, never cut, and the randomizer leaves them alone. Offsets are in mm from the centre between the two lines. Cards are as big as the cards above.
