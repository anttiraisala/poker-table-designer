Molino Royale · Logo Editor

A bigger canvas makes everything look smaller on screen and leaves more room to move things around. Drag any word, or the lines and cards, to move them. Press and drag the middle mouse button anywhere on the canvas to move everything together. The mouse wheel zooms the view toward the pointer, and dragging with the right button pans the view. Holding Space and dragging with the left button pans the view as well, so you move the camera and no object. Ctrl+Z undoes and Ctrl+Y redoes. Drag a rectangle on the empty background to select everything that fits fully inside it. Shift or Ctrl+click adds or removes an item, and Ctrl+A selects all. Dragging or using the arrow keys then moves the whole selection. If items overlap and one is hard to grab, press its Select button in the list. Texts snap to each other's baseline and to the centre by default. Hold Alt (or Ctrl) while dragging to move freely, one pixel at a time. Arrow keys nudge the selection by 1 px, Shift+arrow by 10 px.
