## Testing honesty

The code was written by an AI assistant. What was checked:

- **Node tests** (`tests/geom.test.js`, `tests/solver.test.js`): arc and bulge geometry, offsets, presets with notches, node splitting, zipper triangulation, and the constraint solver including conflict naming. All pass.
- **Browser tests in headless Chromium** (`tests/e2e.js`): tabs, table presets, notch, player-area line with gap, dragging a node, undo and redo, adding and deleting tables, guides, an equal-spacing constraint and dragging under it, a reported conflict, outline-referenced guides, measure tool, guide placement by click, text edits, missing-font warning, importing a real TTF file, source URL, palette add, rename and delete rules, units, real cloth and dim, project save and reload through text, old logo-editor settings conversion, new project, layout sheet, cut export (since V78 with the bundled Clipper; mirror, rotate, strips), PNG export, Blender script download. The randomizer (start, stop, words unchanged, text inside the player area, one undo for a whole run, table shape change, Create casino) is covered too. All pass. The Google Fonts requests fail offline and are ignored.
- **Things on the table** (`tests/e2e-scene.js`): card size modes and centring, chip and card sizes, burn card spacing, seat positions with and without the dealer as a player, 120 random hand snapshots checked against the Hold'em rules, stack spacing, player panel, tooltips, text click-to-select and click-again-to-deselect, the Blender data and the picture option. All pass.
- **Word lists, settings/project split and project tabs** (`tests/e2e-words.js`): the shipped words (Stallion, Knight, Night, Arabia, Arabian Nights, possessives), a project with only a full name and no universal words, the unique union of universal and project words, replacing and resetting the universal list, what the settings file and the project file hold, and tabs (new, duplicate, move, switch, per-tab undo and view, autosave keys, reload, close). Headless Chromium only.
- **Measurements** (`tests/e2e-measure.js`): point to point through the real pointer path with ghost value and snap kind, following a moved table, ray, shortest distance, along a guide, text width, permanent text uids, panel rename and offset, save, undo, broken reference, Esc, toolbar switch. **Not tested, and why:**

- **opentype.js itself.** It is loaded from cdnjs at runtime and could not be downloaded in the development environment; real glyph outlines are untested here. Clipper (bundled since V78) runs for real in the tests, but with simple shapes, not with real script-font glyphs.
- **Google Fonts** (needs the network) and embedding them into the PNG and Blender texture (fetches the font files; may be blocked by the Artifact host).
- **The Blender script.** Blender is not available in the development environment (pypi is blocked, so `bpy` cannot be installed either). `tests/blender_mock.py` runs the generated script against a stand-in `bpy`; it checks the data path, closed solids and the counts of chips, cards and buttons, not the look. The 3D chips, cards, dealer button and the texture without those objects have never been rendered in real Blender. The first version (default table, text logo) was run by the maintainer in preview mode; the project-driven version (outline from data, rail solid, textured cloth, room sized to the table) has not been run. The maintainer reports that he has run the Blender export in real Blender several times and that it works; the newest parts (3D chips, cards and button, ovals) are not confirmed by name.
- **Real browsers other than Chromium, touch input, narrow screens.**
- **The Claude Artifact build** was checked for loading without errors in Chromium only.

## Known gaps

- The poker hand, the pot, the burn cards, the muck and the players are design aids and are deliberately not part of the cut file (decided by the maintainer). The export still prints a warning about the hand; it could be dropped.
- Strip width is typed in by hand (it is not taken from the cutter). The blade offset is approximate near sharp corners and thin parts, and the clip edge of an area export is offset too.
- Keep-out zones are not modelled; the layout sheet and the player-area line only show positions.
- The player-area line gap is an adjustable width centred on one side; the real dealer layout is not known yet.
- The cut export has no registration marks, no bridges for stencil work and no DXF or PDF output.
- Halos of text are approximated by a union of shifted copies (three rings of sixteen), accurate to a few percent of the halo width.
- The halo width is scaled by the mean of the X and Y scale of the export (not per axis); the calibration factor does not rescale halos (a difference below 0.1 mm for factors near 1). The text thin-detail warning is a rough guess.
- The layout sheet is HTML and SVG only (no PDF); `window.print()` is not used because of the Artifact limits. Print it from the downloaded HTML file.
- Blender: gold is not metallic in the texture, curtains and chandelier are simple, and a table is placed at the room centre.

A first-run tour that plays several of them. Examples for the kinds of measurement that need a guide could place a guide themselves.
- **Measurements ("Guides and measurements"; phase 1 built in V54; the Pin button on the temporary Measure tool, Tab to cycle snaps and the perpendicular and quadrant snaps built in V64; the rest below NOT BUILT, build only when the maintainer says so. Note: the ray, shortest-distance and along-guide kinds listed below are already built (phase 1). Still open: aiming a cloth guide at the centre of a real adhesive line (to be thought through); measurements on the layout sheet and in the picture; text arcs as true arcs (they are polylines); design rules, press and roll limits, radius, angle, ordinate, tracking lines, styles, annotations).** A measurement (CAD word: dimension) is a lasting, visible distance between two things, shown in millimetres (or the chosen unit), listed and editable in the Guides tab (to be renamed "Guides and measurements"), draggable (dragging moves the dimension line sideways; the end points stay on their objects), saved in the project, never cut, with a toolbar switch to show or hide all. The existing temporary Measure tool gets a "Pin" button that turns its result into a lasting measurement.
  - **Follows the objects.** A measurement is attached to objects, not to coordinates: when a table, text, line or guide moves or changes, the measurement updates. This needs permanent ids for texts (the current ids `l1`, `l2`... are renumbered when a text is deleted). A measurement whose object was deleted is not removed silently: it stays in the list marked broken (red) so it can be re-attached or deleted.
  - **What can be attached to:** all vector graphics: table outlines and their nodes, rail, player-area line, the two card lines, texts (outline), guides, cloth centre and artwork origin, crossings of any two lines (line with line first; crossings that involve arcs later). NOT chips, cards (including the poker hand cards), dealer button or players.
  - **Snap kinds** are named next to the marker, as in CAD: end, middle, centre, intersection, nearest, perpendicular, tangent, quadrant; Tab cycles when several coincide; Alt or Ctrl still switches snapping off.
  - **Kinds of measurement.** (1) Point to point (aligned, horizontal, vertical). (2) Ray measurement: a start point, a direction (for example towards the centre of a chosen table) and a target line; the result is the distance to the first crossing (example: from a table edge towards the centre to the bottom of the card line). (3) **Shortest distance between two line elements** (works for lines, arcs and text outlines; shows where the two nearest points are and how long the gap is, 0 when they touch or cross; parallel lines have many solutions, so the middle of the overlap is shown). (4) **Along a guide:** the length along a horizontal or vertical guide between two crossings of that guide with other elements (the two crossings are chosen by clicking; they are found again by "nearest to the click" when things move). Later: radius and diameter, angle between two lines, length of an outline or arc (for the Velcro tape path), ordinate measurements (many distances from the same zero point, which fits the layout sheet rule "measure from the centre, never chain").
  - **Text edges.** The outline of a text is found by drawing the text on a hidden canvas at high resolution and tracing the outline (no font file needed), then scaling it to millimetres and turning it with the text. The resolution is an adjustable setting (in the settings, shared by all projects); the default is about 10 pixels per millimetre, so the error is about 0.1 mm for a text 100 mm high, with a cap on the canvas size. A frozen text uses its exact vectors. The trace is cached per text, font and size and redone when they change; it waits until the font has loaded. When the font cannot be drawn, the text box is used and the measurement says so. The cut file keeps using exact outlines from opentype.js (freeze).
  - **Reference first.** Measurements only show a value (shown in parentheses as reference in CAD style). Driving measurements are a later idea and may only move guides, not texts.
  - **Style:** one shared style (text size, arrows, decimals, unit, colour) with per-measurement overrides; groups that can be shown or hidden.
  - **Design rules (suggested, high value):** a minimum or maximum for a measurement, shown green or red (for example text at least 20 mm inside the player-area line; a piece must fit the 380 x 380 mm press area and the 500 mm roll). Ties in with the press and roll guides below.
  - **Layout sheet and picture:** selected measurements can be printed on the layout sheet and shown in the picture (the layout sheet rule "always measure from the centre, never chain" applies to what is placed there).
  - **Other CAD ideas, suggested:** alignment tracking lines (an aid line appears when the pointer lines up with an existing point), annotations with a leader line, ordinate measurements.
  - **Suggested phases:** 1 lasting point-to-point and ray measurements, shortest distance and along-guide, snap names, list in the panel, traced text outlines with the precision setting, permanent text ids; 2 design rules and the press and roll limits; 3 radius, angle, length, ordinate, layout sheet; 4 tracking lines, styles, annotations.
  - **Open:** whether arcs crossing lines are included in phase 1; exact way to pick the two crossings along a guide; where the shared dimension style lives.
- **Players with several visible tables.** Decided: the table that is highest in the table list is the topmost one, as if the viewer looked down through all tables from above; players show only on that table. Needs a way to reorder tables by dragging them in the list (like reordering layers), a decision on how this fits the per-table Show players switch and the Random rows (which work per visible table today), and the list order would also be the drawing order of the outlines.
- **Production pipeline.** The export order is: vectorise the artwork (union and subtraction, halos become holes), split it into cuttable pieces along a grid, name every piece, make a small vector label of the name and place it next to its piece, and cut everything, so the name is cut into the vinyl beside the piece. Pieces are named on the screen too, and an assembly map shows where each piece goes on the cloth. Pieces should preferably fit one heat press; a longer piece can be pressed in several steps, but then a seam remains (a shorter seam looks tidier and is harder to align). The vinyl is single-colour for now.
- **Overlap between pieces.** At every split (strips, tiles, and the parts of the player-area line and other edge lines, curved and straight) the pieces run a little over their neighbours (adjustable, per project), so that a slightly misplaced piece leaves no gap. There is already a strip overlap setting; extend the idea to every split. Halos are no problem for seams: the cut shapes already have the holes, so the split and the overlap are made after the subtraction and only copy visible geometry.
- **Packing long pieces.** Long narrow pieces (edge lines cut into parts) are turned 90 degrees across the roll so that several sit side by side and use little roll length.
- **Roll width and heat press guides.** Design aids on the canvas (toolbar switches): the width of the vinyl roll (from the cutter profile) as guide lines or a band, and a grid of heat-press-sized squares, press size adjustable, default 380 x 380 mm (Stahls' The Maxx Press, confirmed), and the default roll width is 500 mm (polyurethane film roll, 25 m long). The grid starts at the artwork origin by default and can be shifted; it also drives the split into pieces.
- **More guides.** The user should be able to create guide lines for any sensible measure that can be repeated on the real cloth, and use them to align the pieces (the layout sheet already lists guide lines measured from the centre or the outline).
- **Calibration test.** Export the calibration pattern in three variants: a full square, a horizontal part only, or a vertical part only, so that one direction can be calibrated without cutting the whole square. Needs the missing editing screen for cutter profiles and calibration.
- **Guided workflow ("Advise me").** A wizard or advice button that walks a new user through the whole process: creating and placing the texts, checking them against the tables, printing or exporting, cutting and applying. Idea: a checklist that reads the project (missing fonts, texts outside the table, export settings) and links to the right tab, not a locked wizard.
